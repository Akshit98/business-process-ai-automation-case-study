# Resume and interview wording

## Project entry

**Consulting Client Delivery Workflow Optimization & AI Automation**  
Independent portfolio project | 2026

- Analyzed a consulting-operations discovery transcript and mapped nine stages from lead intake to invoicing, including proposal revisions, urgent starts, direct assignments and billing exceptions.
- Documented information gaps and 15 follow-up discovery questions; prioritized five process, automation and AI opportunities with dependencies and human-review controls.
- Developed a proposed future-state workflow, acceptance criteria and a six-metric KPI framework, presented through a portfolio website and supporting case-study documents.

## Interview introduction

I built this project from a consulting-operations assessment. My task was to turn an incomplete discovery transcript into a clear process and identify what still needed to be clarified. I mapped the exceptions instead of showing only the ideal path, then recommended controls for capture, setup, assignments, status reporting and hourly billing. I used rules where the decision was structured and kept AI to drafts a person could verify. The analysis and design are complete; I have not implemented the workflow in a live organization or measured savings.

## What I learned from the analysis

- A named exception is not automatically a broken process. Urgent starts and partner assignments need explicit records and ownership.
- Different client communication formats can be appropriate. Standardize how the format and owner are agreed, rather than forcing every client into one format.
- Fragmented information is a reported symptom; the reason people use alternate tools still needs discovery.
- A billing model changes the control. Missing hourly time should not become a blanket gate on fixed-fee invoices.
